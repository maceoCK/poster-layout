# Poster taste

This is a taste, not a method. Everything in it is real, published work: a board of 510 posters (Dushezz Dashkevich's "Poster inspiration", https://www.pinterest.com/dushezz/poster-inspiration/), downloaded to `references/board/dushezz-poster-inspiration/`, and a set of canonical posters in `references/canon/`. Nothing made by Claude belongs here.

- `img/NNN-<pinid>.jpg`: every pin. NNN is the pin number used below.
- `sheets/`: numbered contact sheets, 40 pins each.
- `categories/tNN-*.jpg`: the looks below, one contact sheet each.
- `pins.json`: source link and description for each pin.

The canon lives in `references/canon/`: `img/` holds 25 posters, `sheets/canon-sheet.jpg` shows all of them, and `canon.json` has the source for each.

The pictures are the taste. The words here only point at them.

## What's liked

- **One idea per poster.** Usually something the type and the image do to each other: a dancer kicking through one letter and passing behind the next, a fish tail growing out of an S, a face cut into the shapes of the letters.
- **Image-led.** Real photographs, photographed props, cutout people, real illustration. Roughly four in five pins carry an image.
- **Real material.** Paper, props, brush lettering, sculpture, staged sets with real shadows.
- **Type with character.** Heavy condensed grotesques, high-contrast Didones, brush and hand lettering, stretched and distorted display faces. One face showing off, one quietly setting the small print.
- **Big color decisions.** One saturated field (orange, yellow, cobalt, red, pink, mint) against a black and white photo, or a full illustration palette.
- **Scale and crop.** Type and images bigger than the sheet and cut by its edges, huge against tiny, generous empty space.
- **Quiet small print.** Dates and credits tucked into one tidy corner block so the idea gets the sheet.
- **Variety.** Each poster has its own voice. A set of options should look like different designers made them.

## The looks

Pin numbers are the clearest examples, strongest first. Each look has a sheet in `categories/`.

**01. Figure x type, depth interlock** (`t01-figure-x-type-depth.jpg`). The subject and the headline share space and trade places in depth: a leg in front of one letter, the body behind the next; a figure sitting on a letter or leaning on a numeral; letters set into a subway door or along a mountain road. 435, 436, 470, 433, 434, 482, 122, 37, 78, 79, 76, 115, 158, 164, 87, 132, 384, 411, 403, 244

**02. Letter as window** (`t02-letter-as-window.jpg`). One huge letter or numeral opening onto an image: food inside a Z, flowers inside a 3, a fish bursting out of an N. Often half solid, half image. 443, 444, 437, 438, 440, 429, 17, 338, 441, 258, 471

**03. Letter fused with an object** (`t03-letter-fused-with-object.jpg`). The letter is made of, or turns into, the subject: an S ending in a fish tail, sausages spelling NO, a French horn wound through NY, a glove gripping the date. 422, 335, 404, 139, 66, 497, 218, 219, 474, 463, 340, 351

**04. Type replaces or covers the subject** (`t04-type-replaces-subject.jpg`). Letters where the face should be, letters cut out of the photo and shifted, a giant vertical word down a face, color bars over eyes, a man tearing the poster open to reveal the number. 421, 430, 116, 278, 272, 431, 366, 385, 375, 361

**05. Sliced, shifted, mirrored** (`t05-sliced-shifted-mirrored.jpg`). A face cut into strips that become books, mirrored heads, a fox and a woman sharing one face in shards, a portrait seen through glass slats. 450, 505, 425, 446, 494, 507, 180, 181, 423, 348

**06. Visual pun** (`t06-visual-pun.jpg`). One image carries the whole message: tree rings as a vinyl record, a sunflower blooming from a trumpet, an origami fox made of an autumn leaf, a hand whose fingers dance a tango. Lots of space, tiny type. 509, 232, 263, 458, 464, 240, 451, 341, 476, 479, 486, 253, 410, 259, 418, 295

**07. Staged set** (`t07-staged-set.jpg`). Real props photographed against flat walls and floors split into two colors: legs coming out of a wall, a person on a purple set with cacti. 92, 93, 247, 1, 9, 60, 346, 18, 274, 408, 271, 241

**08. Black and white cutout collage** (`t08-cutout-collage.jpg`). A cutout black and white figure among flat red, blue and orange shapes, torn edges, thin rules, tiny labels, lots of white. Shapes pass both in front of and behind the figure. 34, 86, 149, 150, 156, 201, 203, 204, 200, 29, 51, 162, 154, 183, 69, 43, 186, 363

**09. Mega type** (`t09-mega-type.jpg`). The type is the image: ultra-heavy or ultra-condensed letters filling the sheet edge to edge, stretched, stacked, mirrored, blurred, hand-painted. 38, 50, 52, 188, 74, 137, 138, 246, 128, 129, 20, 49, 28, 254, 243, 177

**10. Repetition** (`t10-repetition.jpg`). A word, a figure or a photo repeated until it becomes texture, with one break in the pattern. 190, 227, 225, 189, 500, 265, 11, 2, 176, 395, 57, 73

**11. Flat illustration** (`t11-illustration-flat.jpg`). Drawn or cut-paper illustration in saturated flat color, with the lettering drawn into it. 12, 245, 123, 419, 165, 67, 83, 412, 413, 270, 457, 33, 420, 171, 481

**12. Negative space and silhouette** (`t12-negative-space.jpg`). One small, precise object or figure in a big empty field, or a silhouette holding a second scene. 466, 5, 216, 217, 8, 213, 126, 107, 367, 368, 210, 260

**13. Letters through space** (`t13-scattered-type.jpg`). One word's letters spread across the sheet as structure: down a vertical, around a circle, across a football pitch, tied together with thin lines. 63, 62, 108, 249, 262, 182, 276, 374, 508, 415, 478, 376

**14. Duotone** (`t14-duotone.jpg`). A photograph reduced to one ink over a saturated ground, often crossed by big type in the second color. 41, 68, 184, 95, 88, 256, 15, 226
